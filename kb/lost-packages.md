# Lost or Missing Packages

## Marked delivered but not received
First check around your property and with neighbours, then wait 24 hours since carriers sometimes scan packages early. If it still has not arrived, contact support and we will open a carrier investigation.

## Investigation outcome
Carrier investigations take 5-7 business days. If the package cannot be found we ship a replacement or issue a full refund, whichever you prefer.

## Stolen packages
Please file a police report and send us the report number. We handle it as a lost package.
