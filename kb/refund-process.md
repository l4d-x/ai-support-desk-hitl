# Refund Process

## How to request a refund
To request a refund, provide the email used for the order, your Order ID, the amount, and the reason. Only DELIVERED orders are eligible and the refund cannot exceed the purchase price.

## Manager approval
Every refund requires manual approval from a support manager. You will receive an email once a decision has been made.

## Refund timing
Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Cards take 5-7 business days to show the credit; PayPal refunds usually arrive within 2 business days. Shipping fees are refunded only if the item was defective or we sent the wrong item.
