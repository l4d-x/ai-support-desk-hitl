# Damaged or Defective Items

## Damaged on arrival
If your item arrives damaged, email photos of the box and the product within 7 days of delivery. We will send a replacement or refund at no cost, including return shipping.

## Defective within 30 days
Items that stop working within 30 days of delivery can be exchanged for the same product or refunded in full, at your choice.

## Defective after 30 days
After 30 days, defects are handled under the manufacturer warranty. See the warranty policy for what is covered.
