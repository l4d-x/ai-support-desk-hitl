# Shipping Options

## Delivery speeds
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days. Orders placed before 2 PM Eastern ship the same business day.

## Shipping costs
Standard shipping is free on orders over $50 and costs $5.99 below that. Express shipping is $14.99 on every order. We do not ship on weekends or public holidays.

## Where we ship
Domestic shipping covers all 50 US states. PO boxes are supported for standard shipping only.
