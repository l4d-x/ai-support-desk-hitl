# Account and Password

## Resetting your password
Select "Forgot password" on the login page and enter your email. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes. Check your spam folder if it does not arrive within five minutes.

## Changing your email
You can change the email on your account under My Account > Settings. A confirmation link is sent to the new address.

## Closing your account
Contact support to close your account. Order history needed for tax and warranty purposes is retained for 7 years.
