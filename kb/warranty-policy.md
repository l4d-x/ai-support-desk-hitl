# Warranty Policy

## Electronics warranty
All electronics carry a 1-year limited manufacturer warranty starting on the delivery date. It covers hardware defects and faulty batteries that drop below 80 percent capacity within the first 12 months.

## What is not covered
Accidental damage, drops, water or liquid damage, unauthorised repairs, cosmetic wear and loss or theft are not covered by the warranty.

## Making a warranty claim
Contact support with your Order ID and a short video or photos showing the problem. We will arrange a repair, replacement or store credit. Accessories such as cables and cases have a 90-day warranty instead.
