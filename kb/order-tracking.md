# Order Tracking

## Finding your tracking number
A tracking number is emailed as soon as your order ships. You can also find it under My Account > Orders. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear after the label is created.

## Order statuses
PROCESSING means we are preparing your order. SHIPPING means it is with the carrier. DELIVERED means the carrier confirmed delivery. CANCELLED means the order was cancelled and will not ship.

## If tracking has not moved
If tracking shows no movement for 3 business days, contact support with your Order ID and we will open an inquiry with the carrier.
