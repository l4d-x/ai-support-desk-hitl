# Business and Bulk Orders

## Volume discounts
Orders of 10 or more units of the same product qualify for volume pricing. Request a quote by emailing the business team with the product names and quantities.

## Invoicing and payment terms
Approved business accounts can pay by invoice with net-30 terms. A tax-exempt certificate can be uploaded to your business account to remove sales tax.

## Delivery
Bulk orders ship within 5 business days and may be split across several parcels.
