# Product Registration

## Why register
Registering your product links the serial number to your account, which makes warranty claims faster. Registration is optional and does not change your warranty length.

## How to register
Open My Account > My Products, choose Add Product, and enter the serial number found on the box or the back of the device.

## Lost serial number
If you cannot find the serial number, your Order ID is enough to confirm the purchase for warranty purposes.
