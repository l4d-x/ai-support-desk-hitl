# Price Match Guarantee

## How it works
If an authorised retailer lists the identical product for less, we will match the price within 14 days of your purchase. Send us the link and your Order ID, and we refund the difference.

## Exclusions
We do not match marketplace sellers, auction sites, clearance or open-box listings, or limited-time flash sales lasting less than 24 hours. The competitor must have the item in stock.
