# Battery Care

## Extending battery life
Avoid leaving devices at 0 percent or 100 percent for long periods; keeping charge between 20 and 80 percent helps. Do not charge in direct sunlight or in temperatures above 35 degrees Celsius.

## Normal wear
All lithium batteries lose capacity gradually. A battery that drops below 80 percent within 12 months is covered by the warranty.

## Swollen battery
If a device case is bulging, stop using it immediately, unplug it and contact support. Do not attempt to puncture or charge it.
