# Payment Methods

## Accepted payments
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay. We do not accept cash on delivery, cheques or cryptocurrency.

## Payment declined
A declined payment is usually caused by incorrect billing details or a bank security hold. Check the billing address matches your card statement, or try another method.

## Security
Card details are processed by our payment provider and never stored on TechCart servers.
