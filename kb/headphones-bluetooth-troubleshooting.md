# Headphones Bluetooth Troubleshooting

## Headphones will not pair
Put the headphones in pairing mode by holding the power button for 5 seconds until the light flashes blue. Remove any older pairing from the Bluetooth list on your phone, then pair again.

## Audio cutting out
Stay within 10 metres of the device and avoid obstacles. Interference from Wi-Fi routers and microwaves can also cause dropouts. Update the headphone firmware in the TechCart Audio app.

## Factory reset
Hold the power and volume-down buttons together for 10 seconds. The light flashes red twice when the reset is complete.
