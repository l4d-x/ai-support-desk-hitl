# Trade-In Program

## Eligible devices
We accept used smartphones, tablets and laptops made in the last 6 years that power on and are not reported lost or stolen.

## How it works
Get an instant quote online, ship the device free using the prepaid label, and receive TechCart store credit within 5 business days of inspection. If the condition differs from the quote, we send a revised offer and you can decline it for a free return.

## Before you ship
Back up your data and sign out of all accounts. We erase every device on arrival.
