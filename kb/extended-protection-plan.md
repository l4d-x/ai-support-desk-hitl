# Extended Protection Plan

## What it is
TechCart Care is an optional plan that extends coverage to 3 years and adds protection against accidental damage such as drops and spills.

## Pricing and purchase
The plan costs 12 percent of the product price and must be bought within 30 days of purchasing the product. It can be added from My Account > Orders.

## Claims and deductible
There is a $25 deductible per accidental damage claim. Hardware defect claims have no deductible.
