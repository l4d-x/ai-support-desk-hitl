# Order Cancellation

## Cancelling before shipment
You can cancel an order for free while its status is PROCESSING, usually within a few hours of placing it. Contact support with your Order ID as quickly as possible.

## After the order has shipped
Once an order is SHIPPING we cannot cancel it. Refuse the delivery or return it unopened after it arrives, and it will be refunded when it reaches our warehouse.

## Refund for cancelled orders
Cancelled orders are refunded in full to the original payment method within 3-5 business days.
