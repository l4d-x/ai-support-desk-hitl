# Changing Your Shipping Address

## Before shipment
You can change the delivery address while the order is still PROCESSING. Contact support with your Order ID and the new full address.

## After shipment
Once the order has shipped we cannot edit the address. You can ask the carrier to redirect the package using your tracking number, though carrier fees may apply.

## Wrong address supplied
If a package is returned because of an incorrect address, we will re-ship it after you pay the shipping cost again, or refund the order minus original shipping.
