# Privacy and Data

## What we collect
We collect your name, email, shipping address and order history to fulfil orders and provide support. We never sell personal data.

## Your rights
You may ask for a copy of your data or request its deletion by contacting support from the email on your account. We respond within 30 days.

## Marketing emails
Unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of any marketing email. Order and shipping notifications cannot be disabled.
