# International Shipping

## Availability
We ship to more than 40 countries. International shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on weight and destination, and delivery typically takes 7-14 business days.

## Duties and taxes
Import duties, customs fees and VAT are the responsibility of the customer and are collected by the carrier on delivery. TechCart does not control customs delays.

## Restrictions
Products containing lithium batteries cannot be shipped to a small number of countries; the checkout page warns you if your cart is affected. International returns are accepted, but the customer pays return shipping.
