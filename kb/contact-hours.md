# Contact and Support Hours

## Hours
Support is available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern, excluding public holidays. Messages sent outside these hours are answered the next business day.

## Channels
You can reach us by email, live chat on the website, or the support form. We do not offer phone support at this time.

## Response times
Chat is answered within minutes during opening hours. Email replies are sent within one business day.
